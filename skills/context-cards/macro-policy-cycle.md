# Context card: Macro policy cycle (background)

Use when the source uses annual/work-conference macro vocabulary.

| Term | Plain English gloss (background) |
|------|----------------------------------|
| 中央经济工作会议 | Year-end Party–state economic work meeting; often sets next-year priority language |
| 高质量发展 | Quality-focused growth frame — productivity, structure, risk control over sheer speed |
| 新质生产力 | “New quality productive forces” — tech/industry upgrading vocabulary (post-2023 prominence) |
| 稳增长 | Growth-stabilization priority; often coexists with reform and risk language |
| 扩大内需 | Expand domestic demand — consumption/investment framing |
| 积极的财政政策 / 稳健的货币政策 | Standard fiscal/monetary stance labels in public work reports |

## Outlook discipline (hypothesis only)

- Public meeting language signals **priority vocabulary**, not a complete implementation plan.
- Plausible near-term research scenarios often weigh continuity of stated priorities vs. stress themes **only if** the source itself raises them.
- Prefer watchpoints tied to subsequent **public** documents (work report, ministry notices, statistical releases).

**Discipline:** Do not invent unpublished targets or factional motives.
