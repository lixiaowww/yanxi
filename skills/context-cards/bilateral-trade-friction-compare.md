## Compare habit (civilian)

1. Separate **direction language**（主张、呼吁）from **instrument language**（公告、税则、清单、时限）.  
2. If only direction language → keep confidence low; outlook = wait-for instrument.  
3. Optional reader compare: partner-side **public** remedy notices or WTO filings — never invent case numbers.

## Outlook discipline (hypothesis)

- Continuity: more Q&A / commentary without tariffs or lists.  
- Escalation watchpoint: named duty rates, product lists, effective dates in **public** notices.  
- De-escalation watchpoint: joint statements or resumed talks reported by wire/official sites.
