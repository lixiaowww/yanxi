# Context card: Historical analogy discipline (hypothesis only)

Chinese political communication often situates current policies in historical arcs
(reform eras, industrial upgrading slogans). Your job is to **flag possible frames**,
not to assert that "this is a repeat of year X".

## Safe pattern
1. Note the slogan or date marker **as it appears in the source**.
2. Offer 1 short historical parallel as `hypothesis` (e.g. "echoes post-2017 quality-growth vocabulary").
3. State what would falsify the parallel.

## Unsafe pattern
- "Beijing will definitely…" / "this means war/crisis…" without source support
- Collapsing distinct eras into one storyline
- Treating analogy as evidence

**Default stance:** Prefer under-claiming. Analysts add judgment; the tool supplies options.
