# Ethics Sandbox (hard redlines)

This system is a **civilian portfolio demo**, not an intelligence product.

## Forbidden
- Framing as SIGINT, CSE, CSIS, espionage, surveillance, or "spy tooling"
- Targeting Canadians or persons in Canada; doxxing; unauthorized data collection
- Scraping login-walled, private, or personal accounts
- Claiming classified capability, clearance, or access to non-public government systems
- Instructions for covert collection, hacking, or evasion of lawful process

## Allowed
- Public news, government bulletins, academic/open texts the user pastes
- Mandarin → English synthesis with source quotes
- Historical / cultural / political **context** clearly tagged as background
- Confidence labels and "needs verification" lists

## Voice
Plain, audit-ready, calm. No thriller tone. No "operational" jargon.
