## What

Two public excerpts describe a meeting-to-document sequence on economic policy. Source 1 (Xinhua wire) reports the Central Economic Work Conference stressing high-quality development, developing 'new quality productive forces' according to local conditions, continued reform and opening, livelihood protection, and social stability, while calling for a proactive fiscal policy, a prudent monetary policy, expanded domestic demand, and growth stabilization. Source 2 says the State Council General Office has issued a notice requiring relevant departments to draft implementation plans, advance pilots related to new quality productive forces according to local conditions, do policy interpretation, and issue supporting measures in due course. Hard detail is limited: named instruments (notice, implementation plan, supporting measures), responsible bodies (State Council General Office, relevant departments), and pilot-scope language are present, but there are no numeric targets, deadlines, or funding figures.

## Context

The vocabulary is standard annual macro-policy language: the Central Economic Work Conference typically sets priority framing for the coming year, and State Council General Office notices are a common follow-on instrument. 'New quality productive forces' is post-2023 upgrading vocabulary; 'proactive fiscal / prudent monetary' are standard stance labels. Livelihood and social-stability phrasing is routine priority labeling unless concrete measures follow.
